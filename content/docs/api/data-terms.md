## Where the data comes from

| Market | Source | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US stocks and ETFs | Alpaca | trades of every US exchange (SIP), delayed by 15 minutes |
| Crypto | Binance | spot pairs that are trading |

The market data is for signed-in accounts on the beta list. An API key reads it for your account, as the app does.

## What you may do

The stock data comes from Alpaca, whose terms allow it for personal, noncommercial use only.

- **Use it yourself** Backtests, study and research of your own.
- **Keep it to yourself** Do not publish the candles or prices you download, and do not pass them on.
- **No business use** Do not sell the data or build a product on it.
